## 👋 About Me

I'm a Computer Science student at the **Tshwane University of Technology**, based in Witbank (eMalahleni), South Africa. I enjoy turning real, everyday problems into practical software, and I'm building my skills in programming, databases and software design.

I like projects with a real purpose, not just tutorials. My recent work is aimed at student residences: tools that make daily life easier for the people who live there.

- 🎓 Studying **Computer Science** at TUT (since 2024)

- 🏢 Building real-world ideas like **FixFlow** and **ResBites** (see below)
